Support

KITCHENREADY is an independent study app for people preparing for a food protection manager certification exam.

Contact

For questions, bug reports, or purchase help, email bunyamindagdelen@yahoo.com. We usually reply within 2 business days.

Restore a purchase

Open the app, tap the Premium banner, then tap Restore Purchases. You need to be signed in with the same Apple Account you used to buy Premium.

Refunds

Purchases are handled by Apple. To request a refund, go to reportaproblem.apple.com.

About the content

Questions are original and written for study purposes, based on the publicly available FDA Food Code. Requirements can differ by state and local jurisdiction. KITCHENREADY is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by any exam provider, certification body, or government agency, and does not guarantee a passing score.
